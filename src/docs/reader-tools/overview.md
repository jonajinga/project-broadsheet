Project Broadsheet ships with reader tools that most platforms either charge extra for or don't offer at all. They're in the reader panel (click the gear icon in the article toolbar).

## Display

- **Font picker** — 27 font choices (system + Bunny web fonts, lazy-loaded)
- **Font size** — small, medium, large, x-large
- **Line spacing** — tight, normal, relaxed
- **Theme** — light, sepia, cream, dark
- **Reading ruler** — customizable thickness, color, style

## Text-to-speech

- Web Speech API-powered, works in Chrome and Edge
- Word-level highlighting syncs with playback
- Speed control (0.5×–2.0×)

## Annotations

- **Highlights** — six colors, persist in `localStorage`
- **Notes** — rich text attached to any highlight
- **Reading list** — save articles for later

## Focus & productivity

- **Focus mode** — dims everything except the article
- **Voice search** — speak to search
- **Form dictation** — speak to fill any form field

## Portability

- **Import/export** — download all reader data as JSON
- No accounts, no sync servers, no privacy trade-offs
