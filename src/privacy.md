Project Broadsheet respects your privacy. This policy describes what this website collects and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Contact form submissions.** When you fill out a form (contact, book-a-call, quote request, etc.), the information you submit is sent to our email via Web3Forms. We use this only to respond to you.
- **Newsletter subscriptions.** If you subscribe to our Buttondown newsletter, your email address is stored with Buttondown. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.
- **Analytics (if enabled).** We may use Umami analytics, which is cookieless and privacy-first. It records page views and referrers without identifying individual users.

## What we don't collect

- No third-party advertising trackers
- No social media pixels
- No cross-site tracking
- No selling or sharing of your data

## How to reach us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request deletion of your data, email us at <a href="mailto:{{ meta.email }}">{{ meta.email }}</a>.
